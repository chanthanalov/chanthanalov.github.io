01About me

Hi, I'm Chanthana, a second year Mathematics and Computer Science student at the University of Queensland, exploring where technology, mathematics and creativity meet. I love the moment a messy problem turns elegant, whether it's a proof falling into place or a program finally behaving. Studying maths and computer science together lets me chase both: the rigour of theory and the fun of building things people can use.

Away from the desk I train in martial arts and stay active. The mindset carries over: show up daily, refine the fundamentals, and treat every failed attempt as data. I'm hoping to bring these interests together to create innovative solutions and express my creativity.
