Tech4Change · Team Doduo

A satellite-enabled emergency alert system combining culturally familiar warnings with community education for remote communities.

View Solution

01Overview

For our first hackathon, Elyse Ng and I participated in UQ Business School's Tech4Change, where we were challenged to develop a technology solution addressing disaster communication for culturally and linguistically diverse communities across the Asia-Pacific.

We developed a satellite-enabled emergency alert system combining culturally familiar audio and visual warnings with community education to make disaster communication more accessible.

The project was selected as one of eight finalists among 33 teams and received the People's Choice Award!

02How it works

The proposed system combines satellite connectivity, culturally familiar warning methods, and community education to improve emergency communication in remote communities.

  • 01 - Detect & Alert: Emergency information is delivered through a satellite-enabled system, helping reach communities with limited conventional connectivity.
  • 02 - Communicate: Alerts use familiar audio and visual signals designed to be more recognisable and accessible to the communities receiving them.
  • 03 - Prepare: Community education helps people understand the warnings and how to respond before an emergency occurs.

03What I learned

Tech4Change taught me that good technology starts with understanding the people you're designing for. Researching the problem helped me look beyond the technology itself and consider whether a solution would actually be accessible, trusted, and useful to the community.

Joining a Business School hackathon showed me the value of approaching a problem from different perspectives. I learned to combine research, technical thinking, and communication to turn an idea into a solution that could be clearly presented to others.
